**Product Brief:** Work Experience Buddy (WEB)

*Executive Summary*

For both students and first-time job seekers, getting a job is often a rather daunting task. For many, it comes down to that first impression often being the job application and the CV. 
A job application and the CV are used to convey general impression of a job seeker, potential academic background related to the work and any applicable work experience, producing a tailored application. 
In the current market, employers and recruiters use AI and tracking software in order to filter candidates, which can often overlook those with more generic applications and lacking basic recruitment experience.

Work Experience Buddy will be an AI tool that will assist students and first-time job seekers in their search for a job, at the very least leveling the playing field with employers using software to filter them out.
By analyzing the CV and particular job listings, it will help close the gap between a candidate's solid qualifications and the expectations of an employer.
It will help create tailored cover letters, identify missing skills related to the listing's expectations and provide feedback on ATS-alignment, as well as work to make a student more confident in how the job market works.

Work Experience Buddy will make the process of tailoring documents easier, giving the student overall control of their own narrative. 
It will help students be able to show their skills and strengths off in a manner that will appeal to employers. 
With so many different AI-based software used by employers, it is vital that students also have a secure, transparent and intelligent tool that can help them navigate the modern job market.

*The Problem*

The problem faced by students and entry-level job seekers, is often that making a customized application for every job listing is a tedious and long process, often with rejection by the end of it.
They often resort to generic AI prompting, focusing on quantity rather than quality. It results in robotic texts that are immediately dismissed at first glance.
This is because students are completely unaware of how ATS works and how various algorithms value different parts of the application, before ever reaching any human.
It causes students to struggle with presenting themselves, their projects and experience since they do not know keywords used in the industry.
This leads to low callback frequency, burnout from hob searching and an overall dampening of confidence. 
Qualified candidates being filtered out, all due to a lack of keywords, formatting or simply presenting the qualifications in a professional manner.

*The Solution*

Work Experience Buddy will be an AI tool that works to streamline the job application process, while clearing up how algorithms sort through candidates.
Through allowing students to upload their own CVs as well as a wanted job listing, it will help generate custom cover letters, suggest improvements to the CV and provide analyzation of which requirements are met and which aren't.
Students will have control over the rewriting and customization, ensuring that it isn't simply a application made entirely by AI.
It will remake hours of exhaustive work into a clear and confident workflow, helping to submit applications of higher quality and optimization, in a faster timeframe.

*What makes this different*

Work Experience Buddy will be different because it will be transparent, functioning as a recruitment mirror rather than simply generating text. 
It will help remove some of the fog surrounding Applicant Tracking Systems and recruitment evaluations.
Generic tools are often too stylized and easily seen as products of AI.
This tool will take specific experiences like coursework, projects and past jobs, translating it into a language with keywords specific to the job listing in mind.
The user will have full control over rewriting and with a focus on execution speed and focused product design, it will deliver a low-friction workflow that is both intuitive and authentic.

*Who this serves*

The primary users will be students who are looking for a job, whether a part time job during study or a entry-level job after a completed degree. 
It will be for students who want a fast and transparent tool which can protray their projects, coursework and past work experience into a optimized application without loss of their authetic voice.
Success for them will be a higher frequency of callbacks and interviews, with a comparably lesser time spent on cover letters. 
Secondary users will include any first-time job seeker who might only have a high school diploma or very little work experience, seeking a job in the current market.

*Success Criteria*

Success will be measurable based on factors such as user engagement, conversion of applications and system performance metrics. 
A clear indication of success will be a high user task completion rate, positive outcome from user reports, such as a marked increase in callbacks from employeers wihtin in 30 days, fast response time and a retention as users come back to tailore more applications.

*Scope*

The initial scope will focus on the core customization loop such as: securing user authentication, reading the CV in multiple formats, job description ingestion via text or direct link, an automated analysis, customizable cover letter and export.
What is far out of the scope is implementations like interview simulations, automated submissions, extensions for browser and/or mobile apps as well as any form of paid subscription.

*Vision*

If successful, Work Experience Buddy could grow into a end-to-end career launchpad for not only students but also any early career professional. 
It could evolve into a career coach that tracks a candidate's skill growth, recommending learning paths for certain qualification gaps and analyzing a candidate to recommend job listins.
It could bridge the gap between students and those with a degree to the modern job market, becoming an essential toolkit for any student to use to go from education to workforce.
